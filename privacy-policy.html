Privacy Policy

Financial Manager · Last updated 21 July 2026

Summary

Financial Manager helps you view your Israeli bank and credit-card accounts in one place. All processing happens on your device. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process your banking credentials or financial data. We cannot see your data.

What the app accesses

  • Login credentials for the financial institutions you choose to connect. These are entered by you, used only to log in to that institution directly from your device, and stored encrypted on your device using the Android Keystore. They are never transmitted to us or any third party other than the institution you are logging into.
  • Financial data (account balances and transactions) fetched from your institutions. This is stored encrypted on your device so you can see it when you reopen the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Financial Manager has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no backend server. Your credentials and financial data never leave your device except to communicate directly with your bank or card company over their secure (HTTPS) connections.

Where your data goes

  • To your bank / card company: the app logs in and fetches data by talking directly to the institution's own website/API from your device, exactly as your browser would.
  • Nowhere else. No data is sent to EchlonLabs or any analytics, advertising, or cloud service.

Data storage and security

  • Credentials and financial data are encrypted at rest with a hardware-backed Android Keystore key (AES-256-GCM).
  • The app is protected by your device biometric / screen-lock on launch.
  • Sensitive screens are excluded from screenshots and the recent-apps preview.
  • Your data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-transfer.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored credentials and financial data from your device. You can also disconnect an institution inside the app to delete its stored data.

Children

Financial Manager is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
